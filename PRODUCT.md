# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion. The user approved replacing the current Vite/React Router architecture with this stack for the redesign.

## Users

Primary users are people who want to discover and buy Malagasy objects, crafts, and home/lifestyle pieces through a contemporary digital experience rather than a generic catalog.

## Product Purpose

La Maison Malgache presents Malagasy objects, materials, makers, and know-how in one digital house. The site must support discovery, storytelling, product browsing, and purchase intent while increasing the perceived value of each piece through its origin and craft context.

## Positioning

The experience connects four things that should not be separated: the object, the artisan or workshop, the material or technique, and its place in Madagascar. Commerce is the outcome of understanding the object, not the only story on the page.

## Operating Context

Visitors move between editorial discovery and commerce: homepage, shop/category browsing, product detail, collections, savoir-faire, the Maison story, journal content, contact, and cart. Product detail pages should connect commercial information with craft and origin context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the brand name La Maison Malgache.
- Build a new information architecture rather than retaining the current route/component structure.
- Keep useful existing media assets available during migration, but do not treat the legacy UI or static product data as architectural authority.
- The current repository contains placeholder product copy and demo pricing; these are not verified commercial facts and must not be promoted as real claims.
- Checkout provider, payment flow, inventory source, CMS, shipping rules, languages, currencies, and final catalog taxonomy remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: La Maison Malgache.
- Madagascar is central to the product truth, not a decorative theme.
- The approved redesign direction is premium and contemporary, with cultural storytelling and strong imagery.
- Avoid reducing the brand to folkloric decoration or a generic beige "artisan" aesthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing repository assets include a logo, a Madagascar map SVG, a hero image, four product images, a CTA image, and local font files. The current code also establishes broad legacy categories such as pottery, hats, furniture, and bags, but the associated descriptions and prices are placeholders and must not be treated as validated catalog data.

There are no confirmed testimonials, artisan biographies, sustainability certifications, press logos, sales metrics, or verified product specifications in the material reviewed so far. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make provenance part of the product, not an afterthought.
2. Let editorial discovery and commerce reinforce each other.
3. Use fewer, stronger visual moments instead of catalog density for its own sake.
4. Keep product truth and commercial claims verifiable.
5. Design the architecture so catalog, editorial, and maker content can later move to a CMS or commerce backend without rewriting the interface.
